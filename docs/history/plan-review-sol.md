# Independent review of the v2 service-evidence plan

**Verdict: build after fixes.** The storage design and validation work are sufficient to start ingestion. Resolve the four blockers before serving results. For the weekend, prioritize reproducible evidence, safe geographic eligibility and a small set of verified demo queries. Precision claims need labels that distinguish what the record says from what is currently available.

## Blockers

### F01 — Separate evidence of a procedure from readiness

Steps 4 and 6 filter negated hits out of the positive pool but never say what happens when positive and negative items coexist, a direct item is only negated, or a negated direct hit coexists with supporting evidence. The shared negation scan misses post-match text such as “MRI services are outsourced” in a description and can mark an unrelated service as negated across a list item. An outsourced or planned service must not reappear as Unconfirmed through related specialty terms. Retain the matched phrase, its negation scope and the reason for disposition. Make explicit negatives and material contradictions ineligible for recommendation pending confirmation. Scope attribution and negation to the same clause where possible.

D4 and §3.2 allow a procedure explicitly describing a planned or elective C-section, plus a maternity type or unrelated “24 hours,” to become Strong if the negator misses. Even an unambiguously positive “elective caesarean section” with High R and maternity type can pass. Require direct evidence of emergency C-section or locally linked evidence of round-the-clock operative obstetric readiness. Otherwise display the narrower procedure claim. Similarly, §3.3 allows “SNCU” to count twice as direct NICU evidence and infrastructure. Distinguish SNCU from NICU unless the record establishes the requested level. Keep the lower-specificity text visible with its narrower label.

### F02 — Make geographic eligibility explicit

Step 0 can choose the PIN centroid for a 340 km disagreement and assign Low. With Step 7, a query at that centroid yields an apparent road distance of zero and a multiplier of 0.6. A sound dialysis match 50 road km away receives exp(−50 × 1.2 / 25) ≈ 0.091. At equal evidence scores the conflicted record ranks about 6.6 times higher. Remove geographic quality from content reliability as in §0.2 A, while making location a separate eligibility decision. Location error caused by a branch mix-up can also misattribute service evidence. Flag content reliability separately only when the text supports that inference.

Use state/district-qualified city keys and explicit district and PIN lookup. Surface ambiguous place names and reject invalid coordinate pairs or malformed PINs. Define both-vote and zero-source branches, coordinate-only uncertainty when no PIN radius exists, and what state/district to display when resolved coordinates disagree with the PIN directory. A city centroid from facility data can repeat the same wrong coordinate signal. Record vote provenance and prefer an independent locality reference where available.

Choose one hard radius metric and label it. Keep the bounding box as a prefilter and use the exact metric to admit rows. Append each service maximum to its expansion schedule, so 150, 200, 300, 400 and 500 km remain reachable. Stop at the selected range for the slot and fallback. Show estimated distance and location uncertainty separately. Exclude severe or unresolved locations from actionable results, and route other Low cases to an explicitly uncertain list when appropriate.

### F03 — Preserve claim-level provenance

Step 7 joins “item_text, field, source URL,” while the model §2 explicitly says URL provenance is at facility level. A government URL next to a dialysis quote can imply the government page supports that quote when the source data provides no linkage. Label citations as Virtue Foundation record excerpts with field and raw snippet. Display record URLs separately as facility references. Give item-level URLs only when provenance actually exists.

Persist record ID, field, item position or sentence offset, raw item and exact match, matched rule and polarity, template status, selected contribution and rejection reason. Use separate normalized and display text. Store score inputs such as pre- and post-penalty confidence, R components, infrastructure hits and location provenance so the serving row can explain its results. Force the primary direct quote and material caveat into the visible set even when they would miss a top-three list. The interface can show evidence tier, quote, caveats and distance without presenting confidence or capability as calibrated percentages. “Listed in the dataset” and “call to confirm” make the claim boundary clear.

### F04 — Publish one executable contract

Model §3 still multiplies R, confidence and capability, whereas D1 and Step 7 use shrinkage and a capability floor. For R = 0.6, raw confidence = 0.85, base = 0.106, capability = 0.4, high location confidence and zero distance with α = 1, the old raw-confidence interpretation gives 0.204. V2 gives [0.106 + 0.6 × (0.85 − 0.106)] × 0.7 ≈ 0.387. The model also has different field weights, a different Low location gate, old flag codes and no Possible tier.

D4 excludes Low R, incompatible type and merge from Strong, then defines Moderate by “not Strong.” An implementation could send an incompatible direct procedure claim to Moderate before reaching Weak. Decide in this order: resolve evidence disposition, classify eligible support-only as Possible, assign Weak for direct claims failing plausibility/reliability gates, then evaluate Strong, Moderate and direct specialty/description Weak. Define the MRI exception with fixtures for equipment, procedure with a scanner phrase, bare procedure and capability. Specify whether “min size Medium” for cardiac surgery is an eligibility rule, a tier rule or unused. Update the schema with operator type, cluster ID, flags, Possible, score columns and versioned rule settings such as infrastructure weights, code mappings and exclusions.

## Important findings

### F05 — Correlated text saturates noisy-OR

Two procedure items give 1 − (1 − .85)(1 − .425) = .91375. The same sentence copied between procedure and equipment gives .9775. With two items each in procedure, equipment, capability and specialty, the score can reach approximately .99938. Half weight softens a second item but does not address correlated copies. Exact deduplication within a field leaves cross-field copies and paraphrases intact. A template at weight .425 followed by a clean half-weight item at .425 scores .6694. Putting the clean item first and the template second gives .881875 for the same pair. Preserve quote variants for audit, deduplicate cross-field claims for scoring, pick the highest usable weight deterministically, and award second-item credit only for materially distinct claims. Apply the description gate after classifying eligible structured direct evidence, with explicit examples for structured support plus descriptive direct evidence.

### F06 — Shrinkage and fallback need separate rules

The shrinkage derivative with respect to R is raw minus base. For base .106 and raw .075, R = .3 yields .0967 and R = .9 yields .0781. A lower-R record earns a higher confidence value. The issue can also appear for penalized Weak cells. Use shrinkage for eligible direct evidence with a monotone bound such as adjusted = min(raw, base + R × (raw − base)), or use an explicitly monotone discount below the base. Keep supporting-only scores on their own scale. The 0.2 cap on 0.15 × noisy-OR never binds. Name the empirical quantity “direct-hit rate,” estimate it on the fixed deduplicated denominator after v2 filters, and set a burn policy before enabling it. It is a corpus detection rate, not actual prevalence.

Define “fewer than k better results” as a count of eligible direct results after geographic expansion, distinguish Weak direct from support-only, and display Possible separately from ranked recommendations. A radiology code can justify “radiology listed; MRI unconfirmed.” Omit negative, conflicting, incompatible and provenance-contaminated records from this fallback. Require service-specific supporting evidence for time-critical queries or suppress the fallback for those services. Use location and service-specific support to rank within the separate list.

### F07 — Bound and explain capability

D2 does not specify doctor normalization or the result for a zero-term service such as MRI. A site with 500 beds and no infra hits has capability .4 and cap_factor .7 if missing infra is mapped to zero. A 20-bed site with all terms found has size ln(21)/ln(501) ≈ .49, capability ≈ .796 and cap_factor ≈ .898. Either may be a reasonable result for a specific service, but bare terms or documentation density should not yield that advantage by default. On a four-signal dialysis list with weights 1, 1, 1 and .5, ICU alone gives .5/3.5 ≈ .143 and still passes a binary “≥ 1” requirement. If it is the same item as the dialysis claim, Strong is based on only one textual assertion.

Give MRI a documented size-only or neutral policy and define an explicit doctor denominator or use neutral size for the first release. Count each eligible infrastructure signal once and expose missing documentation separately from observed absence. Require qualifying service-specific evidence for Strong. Generic ICU, blood bank, a stand-alone “Level III” or an MRI sentence also mentioning a CT scanner should not qualify independently. Use bounded capability as a tie-breaker until the infrastructure lists pass review. Consider keeping size separate from R completeness, or document the deliberate second use. A 500-bed record currently gains in both terms.

### F08 — Test tradeoffs on pairwise decisions

For evidence-capability products .6 and .4 at equal distance, α = 1 gives a 1.5 ratio and α = 1.5 gives about 1.84, even though both scores decrease. Absolute scores therefore cannot be compared across services. In dialysis, β = 1.2 and d0 = 25 imply an effective decay of 20.83 km. With equal location multipliers, a .3 product at zero distance beats a .6 product beyond about 14.4 road km. At 20 km the latter scores about .230. That may be acceptable for nearby credible facilities. It can surface Weak evidence over several stronger alternatives when distance dominates. The slot only acts when zero Strong results are visible, and may replace a more useful nearby option.

Apply hard credibility and geographic gates first. Make the slot optional for the weekend or specify that the highest-scoring eligible Strong candidate replaces the lowest-ranked returned row, with stable ties and a label such as “stronger documented evidence, farther away.” Keep the higher raw ranking visible or show the Strong row separately to avoid an unexplained order. Test k = 1, k > 1, no Strong and an out-of-range Strong. Use precision targets rather than tier-share quotas, consistent with §0.2 C.

### F09 — Separate independent reliability signals

D3 makes a kitchen-sink flag sufficient for POSSIBLE_MERGE. Its template-plus-kitchen-sink branch is redundant. One kitchen-sink event costs .25 + .5 in consistency and imposes × .7 on raw confidence. With src .8, completeness .5 and recency .5, R falls from .82 to .52. For raw .85 and baseline .106, adjusted confidence drops from .716 to .360 after both penalties, then the cell also goes Weak. This may be warranted with independent evidence of a merge. A single heuristic should not trigger every penalty. Treat KITCHEN_SINK as a review flag, and require a distinct corroborator for severe merge or apply only the largest penalty in a common cause group. Define “names another facility” with a quoted span, known aliases and branch handling. Define templating by deduplicated facility/cluster identity; first-token differences can miss shared names and count unrelated normal boilerplate.

Resolve source defaults explicitly. With tier 5 only, the current formula returns .15 from .2; without a usable URL it has a missing best weight. Use max(0, n − 1) for the domain bonus and a defined no-source default. V5 specifies official-site subdomains and generic tokens like “hospital” are insufficient name evidence. Use domain-boundary checks, proper path parsing, whole distinctive tokens and case-insensitive category exclusions. Specify STATE_MISMATCH severity and exclude all geographic flags from consistency. Apply future-date NULL handling before recency and use a reproducible reference date. Exclude negated and out-of-scope hits from kitchen-sink counts, and keep the validated six-service count until burn passes review. Track the R distribution as an observation, since the floor and cutoff can conceal real heterogeneity.

### F10 — Verify regex behavior in the target Spark runtime

The cardiac lookbehind uses a finite set of fixed-width alternatives and one whitespace character. Its alternatives differ in length, but it has a finite maximum and is not the classic unbounded Java lookbehind problem. It misses “ileal   bypass surgery,” since it checks one space and ileal is absent from the exclusion. It still matches plain “bypass surgery.” Drop the bare pattern, keep coronary/heart/cardiac context, and scope gastric exclusions to the matched context. A record saying “CABG and bariatric surgery” currently loses the CABG hit. The same whole-item suppression can lose general trauma care in a sentence that also names ocular trauma.

Compile the full Java expressions in Spark after stripping Markdown table pipe escapes, apply leading case-insensitivity to every stored pattern including exclusions and negations, and check SQL escaping with actual Delta rows. No obvious catastrophic nested quantifiers are present. Cap scanned text length sensibly, compile patterns once where feasible, and test long text and whitespace. Dialysis misses “haemo dialysis” and “hemodialysis machines” as an infrastructure phrase. Trauma matches “Level I trauma center,” but misses interrupted phrases such as “trauma and critical care centre.” Test generic “casualty” and mixed general and specialty trauma claims as well. For NICU, bind level and equipment phrases to neonatal context. For MRI, bind scanner terms to MRI context and test downstream negation.

Burn §3.7 accepts “burn injury prevention,” “burn treatment workshop,” historic mentions and referrals, while missing plural forms such as “burn units,” “burn injuries” and reversed mentions such as “unit for burns.” The injury regex also catches “burn injury” without any care wording. The sunburn exclusion misses “sun-burn,” and whole-item exclusion can suppress a legitimate burns unit when heartburn appears elsewhere in the same item. Require an affirmative treatment or unit claim and local exclusions. Treat the combined burn/trauma/plastic surgery code as supporting until claim-level validation supports promotion. Add positive, negative, referral, prevention and mixed-item fixtures. Java execution was unavailable locally, so these are static findings and Spark compilation remains a release gate.

### F11 — Make acceptance checks measure what users need

Step 9 gives 40 Strong examples across seven services. At 36/40, the observed 90% has an approximate 95% Wilson interval of 77–96%, and the per-service sample is smaller. Define separate labels for clear offered-service evidence, ambiguous, contradicted or out-of-scope, and real service verified when independent evidence is available. Report the denominator, “can't tell” fraction and service-specific results. Keep “can't tell” from inflating precision through exclusion without disclosure. Use grouped holdout by facility/cluster if fitting any calibration model. A logistic regression on the same small labelled sample is optional and its fitted agreement with evidence labels cannot demonstrate current service availability.

Drop V8′ tier-balance and V15 Low-share quotas as pass criteria. Record them as diagnostics. Add a top-k rubric for scope, quote provenance, emergency readiness, location conflict, fallback labels and empty results. Rerun V12 using full v2 features because that SQL used draft confidence and a location-dependent R proxy. Include a demo query for each input shape, a no-result case, and one record where the system visibly rejects an appealing but unsupported referral. On Free Edition, build persisted Silver/Gold batches, use a broadcast service-rule table and test a full refresh plus the real query entry point early. Defer logistic regression, automated namesake resolution and burn serving if needed. A no-result path with honest coverage is a valid burn demo while validation is pending.

## Keep these choices

- Keep verbatim evidence, facility facts and service-level decisions separately, with a flattened read model for queries.
- Keep exact specialty-code matching, deduplicated facilities, explicit unknowns, source quality checks and list-cap caveats.
- Keep the distinction between evidence of availability, capability and geographic fit. Put geographic eligibility and uncertainty in serving as intended by §0.2 A.
- Keep incompatible-type checks and the real-data-derived off-type heuristic as auditable review signals.
- Keep precision as the tier objective and retain the seven hand-inspected acceptance queries, including an explicit insufficient-evidence result for any service still in validation.
